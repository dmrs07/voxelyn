Voxelyn Survival is an isometric action roguelike. You are a fragile prospector descending into the Vein — an abandoned mine colonized by a fungal-mineral organism that reacts to your presence: it spreads, decays, and attacks.

The loop

Each run lasts 12 to 20 minutes:

  • Enter a procedural region
  • Explore under limited visibility
  • Identify threats by material and sound before they kill you
  • Gather resources and improvise a build from whatever you find
  • Decide: push further for something better, or extract now and keep what you've already earned

The world is the antagonist

Not the monsters — the world. Walls that react, materials that interact with each other, terrain that gets destroyed and becomes a weapon or a trap.

And permadeath is real: nothing you're carrying counts until you make it back out.

The story leaks

It's never handed to you. Everything you learn about the Vein arrives as internal paperwork from Aurix Dynamics, the company that sent you down: asset classifications, containment orders, disposal authorizations, incident reports with half the lines blacked out. The first document never tells the whole truth. Later ones, linked by file code, tell you what the first one refused to.

You declassify the archive by playing:

  • Identify a threat and its asset file opens — keep surviving it and milestones unlock what the company redacted.
  • Make discoveries in the world, and field reports follow.
  • Push your prospector lineage further for clearance earlier generations never had.

No cutscenes. No exposition dumps. Just an archive that slowly stops pretending the mine was ever just a mine.

Bring a friend — optional 2-player PvE co-op.
