TITULO SUGERIDO: Getting the Core is only half the contract
POST TYPE: Game Design
IMAGEM: 063-solo.png (capturada do build daquele dia)

---

For a while the Core was the end of the run. You went down, you found it, you picked it
up, and the game more or less shook your hand. That was the wrong shape for a game whose
whole pitch is that cargo only counts if you get out.

So the Core stopped being the finish line and became the halfway mark.

## The shaft seals behind you

The moment the deepest Core leaves its pedestal, the way down closes. There is no more
descending. The entrance of the sector you are standing in, the hole you dropped through
on the way in, turns into the way up, and you have to cross every sector again, backwards,
carrying the thing you came for.

The world you climb back through is rebuilt from the same derived seed, so the geography
is identical. Same galleries, same rails, same room you remember fighting in.

What is not identical is what lives in it. The Vein did not sit politely waiting for you.
Fauna has repopulated. Salvage sites have re-armed. The route you memorised on the way
down is accurate and no longer safe, which is a much more interesting kind of map than one
that stays cleared.

The resonance you built up stays with you, though. The way back is the proof of the style
you went down with.

## Nothing eases off

Contamination does not drop on the ascent. The Core's own doubled clock keeps running. The
sector you cleared in four comfortable minutes on the way down is a different negotiation
on the way up, with the same layout and worse arithmetic.

Victory only closes on the platform of sector one. Not sector two, not the shaft you came
out of, the actual surface entrance where the run started.

And the escape hatch stays open the whole time: without a Core, abandoning the contract at
any depth still works, exactly as before. Leaving early with a bag of ore has always been
a legitimate way to play, and turning the Core into a one way door would have quietly
deleted that.

## The canaries

The other half of this update is decoration that is not decoration.

The Aurix infrastructure kit hangs canary cages through the mine, and they are the live
contamination meter. When contamination crosses the threshold, every canary in the world
goes quiet on the same tick, reading the same authoritative number the HUD reads. Not a
visual approximation. The same value.

They are also the only yellow allowed anywhere in the environment art, because in this
game yellow means information. If a wall could be yellow, a dead canary would just be
scenery. The variant axis in the atlas is not colour or damage or age. It is state, alive
or dead, on purpose.

It means you can learn to read the room instead of the number. Walk into a gallery, hear
nothing, and know exactly how much the clock has eaten before you look up.

## Why bother

Because the interesting decision in an extraction game is never "should I take the
treasure". It is "how much further do I push before the walk back stops being survivable".
That decision does not exist if picking up the prize ends the level.

Now every step down is a step you have to pay for twice.
