# Surface material separation

## Wet fungal carpet

Ground biomass is damp. Heat first dries it into a visible smoking state. Only after the drying timer completes does it ignite, and its fuel lasts longer than volatile gas.

## Spore cloud

The Spore Bomber leaves an organic green cloud. It remains localized, expires naturally, harms prospectors, and burns briefly without exploding.

## Sulfur gas

Mineral vents emit yellow sulfur gas. It diffuses, is toxic, detonates immediately under heat or electricity, and the resulting flame disappears quickly.

The three materials must remain distinct in authoritative state, runtime particles, baked surfaces, fallback colors, tests, and compatibility versions.
