Motion Principles

Anticipation

Every attack winds up 2–3 frames backward before striking. Reads as intent — player learns the telegraph.

Squash & stretch

Idle bob compresses 2px on down-beat. Attack stretches forward 3px then snaps back with a smear frame.

Silhouette first

Lamp, shoulder, weapon must read against black at 32×32. Color is secondary.

Iso footprint

Feet, shadows, and attacks sit on the same diamond floor plane, so every facing keeps the 2:1 read.
